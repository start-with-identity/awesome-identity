# Identity Vendor Directory

Part of [Awesome Identity](README.md). Commercial identity products grouped by primary category.

- Each vendor appears once, in the category that best describes its main product.
- Order is alphabetical. There are no featured, sponsored, or ranked rows.
- Descriptions say what the product does, not how good it is.
- Pricing shows the entry model only: `Commercial` paid, `Freemium` free tier with paid plans, `OSS` open-source core with commercial offerings.

Vendors can submit or correct their own listing under the same rules as everyone else. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
