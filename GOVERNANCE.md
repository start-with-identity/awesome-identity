# Governance

Awesome Identity is run in the open by the [Start With Identity](https://startwithidentity.com) community. This page explains who decides what and how disagreements are settled.

## Maintainers

Maintainers review pull requests, triage issues, and keep the list healthy. The current maintainers are the members of the [start-with-identity](https://github.com/start-with-identity) organization with write access to this repository.

Regular contributors who review well and know the field are invited to become maintainers. If you would like to help maintain the list, say so in [Discussions](https://github.com/start-with-identity/awesome-identity/discussions).

## Reviews and merging

- One maintainer approval is enough to merge.
- A maintainer does not approve a PR that adds or changes a listing for their own employer or for a direct competitor. Another maintainer reviews it instead.
- Vendor self-submissions are welcome and follow the same rules as everything else.
- When a PR is declined, the reviewer points to the rule in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) that it does not meet.

## Neutrality

- No entry is paid for, featured, highlighted, or ranked.
- No sponsorship slots, affiliate links, or referral parameters, ever.
- Start With Identity's own resources follow the same rules as everyone else. They are not listed in the tool or vendor sections, and only a small number of unique public datasets may appear in the data section.
- If the project ever accepts donations, the money pays for maintenance only and buys no placement.

## Disagreements

Most disagreements get settled in the PR or issue thread. If that does not work, any participant can ask for a maintainer vote in the thread. A simple majority of maintainers who respond within seven days decides. Maintainers with a conflict of interest abstain.

## Changing these rules

Changes to this file, to [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), or to the inclusion criteria go through a pull request that stays open for at least seven days so people can comment.

## Contact

Use [issues](https://github.com/start-with-identity/awesome-identity/issues) or [Discussions](https://github.com/start-with-identity/awesome-identity/discussions) for anything about the list. For code of conduct reports or anything private, email community@startwithidentity.com.
